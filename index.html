// questions

Straight answers.

What changes after the optimize?

Security reviewers find their answers on the listing instead of emailing you. Procurement can see what one unit buys. AI search can tell who the product is for and what it does. Re-run the audit here and you see the new score.

What do you need from us?

Your listing URL, product website, pricing, and a one-pager or deck if you have one. Anything you can prove, like certifications, integrations or customers, goes into the listing. Anything you can't prove stays out.

Do you need access to our AWS account?

Not for the optimize: you paste the new copy yourself. For a launch, you choose: grant temporary IAM access that you revoke once the listing is live, or I hand your team every field ready to enter.

Who builds the SaaS integration?

Your engineers. AWS requires SaaS products to connect for sign-up and billing, and that is code in your product. I handle the listing, the pricing setup and the AWS approval.

Our product isn't an AI agent. Does this apply?

Yes. Six of the seven dimensions apply to any product on AWS Marketplace. Protocol & category fit only counts if you tick "AI agents call this product". Otherwise it is left out and your score is out of the 60 points that apply, so you never lose points for it.

Is this an official AWS score?

No. It is my own audit of the listing text, using the same fixed checks on every listing. It shows what a buyer, reviewer or AI search can find on the page. It does not predict AWS approval, search placement or sales.

How is the benchmark built?

I read live AWS Marketplace listings through the AWS Marketplace Discovery API, keep at most two per seller so large sellers don't skew it, and score each one with this same audit. The last refresh was 24 September 2026. Only aggregate numbers are published, never a single listing's score.

Why not let AI rewrite the listing?

AI drafts words. It doesn't know which reviewer questions your listing leaves open, or which claims you can prove. Every line I write uses your facts only.
